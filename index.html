About

Research across language, vision, reasoning, and real-world AI systems.

My trajectory connects foundational NLP research with multimodal AI and production-facing industrial research, with a consistent emphasis on knowledge grounding, reasoning, reliability, and human-AI collaboration.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Patna, where my thesis focused on enhancing medical consultations through knowledge-grounded question answering and dialogue generation.

I later joined the Center for Research in Computer Vision (CRCV), University of Central Florida as a Postdoctoral Scholar, working on multimodal medical AI, medical visual question answering, causal reasoning, and vision-language systems.

Most recently, at IIIT Bangalore in collaboration with Siemens, I worked on industrial AI, including PLC/SCL program modeling, knowledge-guided automation, agentic coding systems, and efficient LLM context/token usage for real software-engineering workflows.
